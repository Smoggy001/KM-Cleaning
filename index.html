Questions

Before you book.

The short answers. Anything else — call, text, or email and we'll sort it out directly.

How much will it cost?

There's no fixed price list — every property and job is different, so we quote based on what you tell us in the booking form. Quotes are free, with no obligation to book.

How do I know my booking is confirmed?

We review every request personally and reply within one business day — with a quote and confirmation, or, if we can't fit it in, an apology and an alternative time where one's available.

What areas do you cover?

Bookings are taken from anywhere in Australia.

What are your hours?

Monday to Saturday, 7am to 6pm. Requests submitted outside those hours are reviewed the next business day.

Do you offer one-off cleans, or only regular bookings?

Both — from a single one-off clean to a regular schedule, whatever suits your property.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Yes — just call, text, or email us and we'll find a time that works.
